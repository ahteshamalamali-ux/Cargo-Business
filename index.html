Frequently Asked Trade Questions

To export from India, you primarily need an Import Export Code (IEC) issued by the DGFT, an Authorized Dealer (AD) Code linked to your bank and port of export, GST registration, RCMC (Registration cum Membership Certificate) from the relevant Export Promotion Council, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Bill of Lading/Airway Bill. Bharat Trade Solutions handles all these setup processes end-to-end.
With prior documentation filing (Faceless Assessment & Advance Bill of Entry), standard sea cargo is cleared within 24 to 48 hours of vessel discharge at major ports like JNPT, Mundra, and Chennai, avoiding demurrage and detention charges. Air cargo clearance can often be completed within 12-24 hours.
Yes! We ensure your Shipping Bills are filed with accurate HNS codes and claim flags so that government incentives like RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products), Duty Drawback, and GST refund scrolls are seamlessly generated and credited directly to your bank account.
The Harmonized System of Nomenclature (HSN / HNS) is an international 8-digit product code used to determine customs duty rates, GST, import policies, and regulatory permissions. Incorrect HNS code declarations can trigger heavy penalties, customs seizures, or delays. Our CHA experts double-check every item before filing.
